Research with Dr. Sudhir Ranjan Jain, IIT Delhi

After meeting Dr. Jain following a talk in Gurgaon on fluid dynamics, I began a year-long research apprenticeship exploring landscape functions and spectral theory. The collaboration produced three arXiv/Zenodo papers and the Chandra-Jain Conjecture.

May 2024

Euler Circle

Within eighteen months I completed my school's four-year high school mathematics curriculum under Dr. Saugata Ghosh. In tenth grade I applied to Euler Circle's Cryptography 2024 and IPRW 2024 programmes.

I studied real analysis under Ari Krishna — Princeton Lectures in Analysis, measure theory, Borel σ-algebras, and introductory stochastic processes. My research paper explored the Hausdorff dimension through measure theory; I later extended this to Brownian motion and presented the work to 30+ Euler Circle students, TAs, and faculty.
