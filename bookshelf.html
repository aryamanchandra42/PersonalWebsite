Bookshelf

Books I keep coming back to.

Not a full library — just the ones that changed how I think, in no particular order.

  • Gödel, Escher, Bach

    Douglas Hofstadter

    The book that made self-reference feel like the most natural thing in the world.

  • The Feynman Lectures on Physics

    Richard Feynman, Robert Leighton, Matthew Sands

    Still the clearest explanation I've read of why anything works at all.

  • A Mathematician's Apology

    G. H. Hardy

    Short, a little defensive, and completely right about what makes a proof beautiful.

  • Surely You're Joking, Mr. Feynman!

    Richard Feynman

    Proof that curiosity is a personality trait, not just a research method.

  • Gulliver's Travels

    Jonathan Swift

    Satire that still lands, three centuries later.
