Live the Learning Guide

01

Research into a practical guide

Evaluated 30 education models to author and publish a 9-module experiential learning guide for CBSE & ICSE schools; mentored by Mr. Sunil Singh, International Schools Partnership Ltd.

02

Adopted across Haryana

Government Education Officers confirmed the guide’s official alignment with NEP 2020. Ten schools across Haryana formally adopted it as an educational curriculum, reaching over 8K students. Saksham · assistive technologyThree patent applications

Assistive communication glasses

Smart glasses that turn printed text and lip-read speech into tactile Morse-code messages.

A scene classifier switches between text recognition and visual speech recognition. The glasses send recognized words through temple vibrations, with adaptive Morse-code pacing and priority handling for urgent messages.

Application No. 202611103635

Wearable communication cap

A cap-based visual communication aid for deaf and hard-of-hearing users.

The application describes lip-reading alongside visual emotion cues, with information delivered through haptic alerts and optional bone-conduction audio.

Application No. 202611103628

Multi-zone tactile navigation stick

A cane that communicates object type, distance, and direction through haptic zones in the handle.

The application describes camera and depth sensing, object prioritization, and distinct alerts for ground-surface hazards.

Application No. 202611120625
